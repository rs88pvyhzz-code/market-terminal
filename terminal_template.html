SOURCES AND DATA QUALITY

This is a static HTML snapshot, generated by Python. To refresh, rerun python build_terminal.py. The HTML itself never fetches new market data. Yahoo Finance chart data and FRED graph CSV are unofficial download interfaces and may become inaccessible. A successful refresh does not guarantee an exchange-quality quote.

Price-only comparisons exclude dividends unless an adjusted-close mode is selected. GBP comparisons convert with historical spot FX; adjusted-close figures are only total-return proxies. All modes exclude taxes, trading costs and futures roll mechanics. ETF distributions and index methodology also matter. A rise in a bond yield is not a return on a bond; yields are reported as changes in bp. For investment decisions, verify with primary/official market data.
