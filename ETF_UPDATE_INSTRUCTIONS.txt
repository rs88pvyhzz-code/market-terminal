Do not replace install_etf_trackers.py or your original dashboard.

Why this works:
- The workflow first runs the EXISTING install_etf_trackers.py.
- It then runs apply_etf_issuer_data.py --repo .
- The patch updates etf_catalog.csv and hooks the snapshot enrichment
  immediately after the issuer feed and before terminal.html generation.
- Existing issuer data have priority over dated screenshot fallback.
- Fund size is identified by its real issuer fund, ISIN, currency and
  reporting date. Uncertain AUM reporting dates are marked as such.
- The patch is idempotent and makes an initial CSV backup on its first run.
- Original company news, analytics and market panels remain unchanged.
